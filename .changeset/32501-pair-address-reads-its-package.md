fix(metadata-protocol)!: a pair address (`?package=<base>.custom`) reads the package it names, so its envelope is that package's and an App pair two of whose modules hold the name is refused naming both, never answered by the first registered holder (ADR-0150 D5, #32501)

Clause-②: yes (narrowing) — `GET /meta/:type/:name?package=<App>.custom` and its `/layers`, on both transports, when two enabled modules of the App hold the name, are refused `409 RESOURCE_CONFLICT` naming both modules where they served the first registered module's item; `/layers` at an App pair that holds a stored row of a name two of its modules ship, when one of those modules is the first registered package that ships the name, is refused the same way where it reported that module's body as the code layer. The other moved answers keep their status and name the package the address reads in place of the first registered holder: the envelope (`_packageId`, `_packageVersion`, `_provenance`) of `?package=<base>.custom` and of a stored row read at an exact address. Three widen from 404 to 200, on the plain read and `/layers`: the pair of an enabled base and an App pair one of whose two module holders is disabled, when the disabled holder registered first, and a row of a package that ships nothing beside a disabled shipper.
Metadata-protocol: none
Skills-docs: none — `skills/**` and `content/docs/**` teach no read at a customization pair address of a name two packages hold; the prescription ships in the refusal text and the before → after list below.

**BREAKING** answer narrowing at the `/meta` exact read (`@objectstack/metadata-protocol`), for the pair address of an App two of whose modules hold the name.

- **Before → after, served → refused.** `GET /meta/:type/:name?package=<App>.custom` and `GET /meta/:type/:name/layers?package=<App>.custom`, two enabled modules of the App holding the name, measured on the `SchemaRegistry` composition the transport pins drive: 200 with the first registered module's item → `409 RESOURCE_CONFLICT`, `ambiguous-in-closure`, naming both modules. The real ObjectQL engine already refused the plain read. `/layers` at the App pair when the pair holds a stored row and two modules hold the name, one of them the first registered package that ships it: 200 with that module's body as `code` → the same 409; the plain read keeps serving the row, which is the pair's own and unambiguous, while the code layer has two holders. With another package registered ahead of the modules, `/layers` there already answered this 409. First-party callers: 0.
- **Before → after, same status, another package named.** `?package=<base>.custom`, another enabled holder of the name registered first: the base's body under the other holder's `_packageId`, `_packageVersion` and `_provenance` → under the base's. A stored row at an exact address: the base's override row at its pair, the same; a row bound only to a package that ships nothing, under the first registered shipper's envelope → its own stamp, no artifact envelope; the App pair's own row beside two module holders, under the first registered shipper's envelope → its own stamp. `/layers` at the base's pair and at a package that ships nothing already answered the base's code layer, or `code: null`, and does not move. First-party callers: 0.
- **Before → after, widening.** `?package=<base>.custom` and its `/layers`, the base enabled and another holder of the name disabled and registered first: 404 → 200 with the base's item. `?package=<App>.custom` and its `/layers`, one of two module holders disabled and registered first: 404 → 200 with the enabled module's item. `?package=<P>` and its `/layers`, where P ships nothing and holds a stored row of the name, its only shipper disabled: 404 (withheld by the disabled shipper's grafted stamp) → 200 with the row under its own stamp. First-party callers: 0.

**The remedy.** Read an item two modules of an App hold at the module's own address (`?package=<module>`), as the refusal names.
