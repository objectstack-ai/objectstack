fix(plugin-audit)!: the audit sealer seals a window into checkpoints of at most 10,000 rows each, `os audit verify` fails on a row two checkpoints list, and a pass bounds what it re-reads while a chain stays skipped

Clause-②: yes (narrowing) — narrowed: `os audit verify` reports a row id that two checkpoints list as the new chain fault `listed_twice` and fails the run, where the 18.4.0 verifier accepted it, and the 18.4.0 sealer could write such a chain when two of its late sealings overlapped. Widened: the checkpoint's signed body gains two optional fields, `part_index` and `final_part`; `sys_audit_checkpoint` gains two nullable columns for them; and the exported `AuditChainFaultKind` union gains `listed_twice`.
Metadata-protocol: none

**BREAKING** for `os audit verify` on a chain that an 18.4.0 sealer wrote while two of its late sealings overlapped (two replicas with no cluster service, whose lock is in-process only, or a lease that lapsed). Such a chain lists one late row in two checkpoints. Verify now reports it as a `listed_twice` fault and fails, where it passed. No tolerance and no repair ships for it. A chain one sealer wrote at a time is not affected.

**Bounded checkpoints (ADR-0154 D4, as amended 2026-11-14).** A chain's rows of one window now seal into consecutive checkpoints of at most 10,000 rows each, cut in `(created_at, id)` order, instead of one checkpoint however many rows the window holds. Each checkpoint has its own seed. Its signed body carries two new fields, `part_index` (from 0) and `final_part`, and `sys_audit_checkpoint` stores them in two new nullable columns. A pass that stops in the middle of a window resumes it on the next pass as further `window` checkpoints, never as `late` ones. Late rows split into checkpoints of at most 10,000 rows the same way. The work and memory of one checkpoint no longer grow with the window. A checkpoint written before this release has neither field. It still verifies, reads as one final part, and its chain goes on. `part_index` can skip a number, and a window can end with no final part: a part whose rows were all deleted or moved between the sealer's two reads of the window is not written.

**`os audit verify`.** A row id that two checkpoints list is now a fault of the new kind `listed_twice`, and the run fails. The second listing holds nothing for that row. Verify does not check that a window's parts are continuous or that it ends in a final part. The chain links show a removed interior part, as a `gap` or a `link` fault. They do not show a removed newest part; that part's rows read as late and unsealed instead.

**Skipped chains.** While a chain stays skipped by a chain-local fault, it holds back the sealed-through watermark, so each pass walked every window from there and re-read all of them. Now, while a chain is skipped, a pass walks at most 168 windows that seal nothing (one late horizon) and then stops. When it stops, the sealer says so at `error`, naming the skipped chains. Once no chain is skipped, the walk is not cut, so sealing catches up whether the chain's fault was fixed or the chain has nothing left to seal.

**Upgrading is one way once this release writes a checkpoint.** An 18.4.0 reader does not know `part_index` and `final_part`. The 18.4.0 verifier drops them and then fails the signature of every checkpoint that carries them. An 18.4.0 sealer computes the chain head's digest without them, so the checkpoint it writes next is a `link` fault. Roll back to 18.4.0 only before this release seals its first checkpoint. A host's checkpoint sink receives the two fields in each body.
