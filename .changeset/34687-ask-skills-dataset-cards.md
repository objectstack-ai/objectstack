feat(scenarios): an opt-in skills directory for ask turns, and dataset questions on two open cards

Clause-②: yes (widening) — new CLI flag `--ask-skills` on `objectstack-scenarios run` / `run-scenarios`; the card schema accepts the optional keys `expect.datasets` and `run.datasetQuestions`, with new exports `DatasetExpectationSchema`, `DatasetMeasureExpectationSchema`, `DatasetDimensionExpectationSchema`, `DATASET_MEASURE_AGGREGATES`, `DatasetMemberRefSchema`, `DATASET_QUESTION_KINDS`, `DatasetExpectedRouteSchema`, `DatasetQuestionSchema` and the types `DatasetExpectation`, `DatasetQuestionKind`, `DatasetExpectedRoute`, `DatasetQuestion`, and new optional fields `ScenarioExpect.datasets` and `ScenarioCard['run'].datasetQuestions` (`.`); new exports `EvalGraphDataset` and `EvalGraphDatasetMember`, and `EvalGraph.datasets` items gaining optional `object` and `members` (`./score`, `./score/full`); new exports `DATASET_GRADERS`, `OBJECT_QUERY_TOOLS`, `statedFigures`, `labelsFallback`, `gradeDatasetQuestion`, `gradeCardDatasetQuestion` and the types `DatasetGrader`, `DatasetGrade` (`./score/full` only); new exports `datasetLines`, `datasetQuestionPrompt`, `datasetTally` and `DatasetQuestionResult`, new optional fields `ClaudeCodeDriverOptions.askSkillsDir`, `DriverInfo.askSkills`, `RunHalf.datasetQuestions` and `HalfScores.datasets`, and `TABLE_DIMENSIONS` gaining `'ask skills'` (all `./runner`). `scoreGraph` (`./score`, `./score/full`) and `scoreScenarioArtifact` (`./score/full`) answer differently on a card that declares datasets: they add `dataset:` and `dataset-measure|dimension:` checks, so that card's Build total grows.
Metadata-protocol: none

- **`--ask-skills DIR`** (live `claude` driver only, off by default) places one skill directory, or a directory of them, at `.claude/skills/NAME/` in every question and operation turn's working directory and allows the `Skill` tool beside `mcp__app`. Authoring turns never see them. The record's driver states which skills the turns had: per skill its name, the sha256 of its files, and the front-matter `version` when it has one. `summary` refuses to sum runs with different skills. Off, the turn's arguments and the record are unchanged.
- **Dataset questions.** A card may declare `expect.datasets` (measures with synonyms and descriptions) and `run.datasetQuestions` of three kinds: `metric_selected`, `asked_back` (ambiguous or near-miss) and `fallback_labelled`. The loader holds each word to what the datasets declare, and checks each dataset with the platform's `DatasetSchema`. The Build half checks the datasets. The Run half asks the questions and grades the route each answer took (`metric-selected`, `asked-back`, `fallback-labelled`) on a new `Run · datasets` scorecard row.
- `order_to_cash` and `travel_expense` carry datasets and five dataset questions each.
