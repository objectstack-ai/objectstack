The allowance table below applies to every trip that starts after the policy date.

| City tier | Lodging per night | Meals per day | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | 600 | 100 | Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, Shenzhen |
| Tier 2 | 450 | 80 | Provincial capitals and planned cities |
| Tier 3 | 350 | 60 | Every other prefecture-level city |
| Overseas A | 1800 | 300 | North America, Western Europe, Japan |
| Overseas B | 1200 | 220 | Southeast Asia, Eastern Europe |
| Overseas C | 900 | 180 | Every other country and region |

- Receipts are required for lodging. A missing receipt is reimbursed at half the tier rate, with a written explanation signed by the traveller.
- Meal allowances are paid per calendar day, without receipts.

Claims are filed within thirty days of return.
