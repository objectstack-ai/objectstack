## ObjectStack Documentation

**The ontology is the software.** One executable business ontology. AI writes it, the
runtime runs it, agents operate it, you own it. What the word means here — and what it
does not — is [Business Ontology](/docs/concepts/ontology).

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["Describe<br/>in plain language"] --> B["Claude Code authors<br/>typed metadata"]
    B --> G{"os validate<br/>the gate"}
    G -->|fails| B
    G -->|passes| V["You verify in<br/>the Console"]
    V -->|iterate| A
    V -->|ship| M["MCP — your app<br/>is AI-operable"]
```
