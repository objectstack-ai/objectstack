1. **1280 px window:** the console sidebar mounts as the icon rail, and the content is window − 48 px.
2. **Expand over content:** a click on the rail background, the app bar's sidebar button or ⌘B / Ctrl+B expands the full sidebar over the page, and the content does not move. A navigation choice, a press outside or Esc folds it again. A rail icon click still opens that entry, and hover does nothing.
3. **Keep sidebar open:** while the sidebar is expanded, its foot shows the new item. Pressing it keeps the wide sidebar on a narrow window across a reload, and pressing it again brings the fold back.
4. **1440 px:** nothing changes. Below the phone breakpoint the drawer is unchanged.
5. **The wide-window state is kept:** collapse on a wide window, narrow it, then widen it again; the collapsed choice comes back.
